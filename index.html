Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Ariya

What is Ariya?

Ariya is a free, modern Android music player with a beautiful interface. It supports synced lyrics, FLAC lossless audio, playlists, queue controls, crossfade, gapless playback, and music discovery from multiple sources.

Is Ariya free to download?

Yes. Ariya is free and open source. You can download the latest APK from the official GitHub releases page.

Which Android versions does Ariya support?

Ariya requires Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later and supports arm64, armv7, x86, and x86_64 architectures. It needs about 30 MB of free storage.

Does Ariya support FLAC and lyrics?

Yes. Ariya supports FLAC lossless audio and synced lyrics that follow along with playback. Audio quality can be selected from low to ultra-high depending on the source.

Is Ariya available on iOS?

No. Ariya is currently available for Android only. iOS support is not available at this time.

How do I report a bug or get support?

Join the Ariya Discord support server, or use the Report a Bug form on the Support section. Reports go straight to the team.

Is Ariya open source?

Yes. Ariya is open source and its code is publicly available on GitHub under the MIT license.
