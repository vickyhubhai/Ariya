Features

Everything you need for a complete music experience

▶

Music Playback

Smooth, high-quality audio playback with gapless and crossfade support for an uninterrupted listening experience.

🔍

Music Discovery

Discover new music through multiple audio sources and explore trending tracks, new releases, and curated playlists.

♫

Lyrics

Follow along with synced lyrics while you listen. Never miss a word of your favorite songs.

🎵

Playlists

Create and manage your own playlists. Organize your music your way with easy playlist management.

♪

FLAC Support

Enjoy lossless audio with FLAC support. Auto-check for the best available audio quality including hi-res sources.

⚙

Audio Quality

Choose from multiple audio quality options from low to ultra-high. Detailed codec and bitrate information when available.

♩

Queue & Controls

Full queue management with drag-to-reorder, play next, shuffle, repeat, and seamless track transitions.

🌐

Multiple Sources

Access music from multiple audio sources. Find the best version of any song across different providers.

💬

Discord Integration

Share what you're listening to with your Discord community through rich presence integration.
